Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

Atlas Shorts Studio is a local video preparation and publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @atlas_alias.

Data used

When a user authorizes the app, it may access basic TikTok profile information and the permissions needed to upload or publish videos. The app processes only videos selected by the user.

How data is handled

Video processing occurs on the user's device. Videos and publishing metadata are sent directly to TikTok through TikTok's official APIs. Atlas Shorts Studio does not sell personal data and does not operate an independent advertising or analytics system.

Storage and deletion

Local output files and authorization credentials remain under the user's control on their device. Users may delete those files, revoke the app in their TikTok settings, or contact @atlas_alias on TikTok for support.

Third parties

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy when its services and APIs are used.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact @atlas_alias on TikTok.
